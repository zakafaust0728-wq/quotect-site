SnipPocket Support

How to contact us about SnipPocket. Frequently asked questions will be added to this page when the App is released.

Contact

Please contact us by email.

support@quotect.jp

You can also use “Send feedback or report a problem” in the App’s settings. It goes to the same address.

Including the following helps us look into it faster:

  • Your iPhone model (e.g. iPhone 16)
  • iOS version (Settings → General → About)
  • SnipPocket version
  • What happened, and what you did just before
  • A screenshot, if possible

It may take a few days for us to reply. We reply in English or Japanese. Your email is used only to respond to your inquiry.

Purchases and refunds

  • The yearly plan renews automatically every year. You will be charged unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.
  • You can cancel in the iPhone Settings app → your name → Subscriptions.
  • The one-time purchase is a single payment with no recurring charges.
  • If you change iPhones, tap “Restore purchases” in the App.
  • To request a refund, use Apple's “Report a Problem” (reportaproblem.apple.com).
