SnipPocket Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Quotect LLC (“we”) handles information in the iOS app “SnipPocket” (“the App”).

1. We collect no information

The App does not connect to the internet. Everything you save in the App, and your settings, are stored only on your device and are never sent to us or to any third party. The App has no ads, no account, no analytics and no tracking.

2. Information used on your device

The App uses the camera and your photos only to recognize text and to show thumbnails. Text recognition, including text in photos, happens entirely on your device, and nothing is sent outside it. The App does not use the microphone.

3. Information handled by Apple

Purchases and refunds are handled by the App Store, and we do not receive your name or payment information. If you have turned on “Share With App Developers” in your iPhone settings, crash reports that do not identify you may reach us through Apple; we use them only to fix bugs.

4. Contacting us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you and to fix problems, and we do not share them with third parties except as required by law. You can also contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of this information.

5. Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the changes on this page.
